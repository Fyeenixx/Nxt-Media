About

A leaner kind
of ad agency.

NXT Media exists because most agencies make ads complicated on purpose — to justify their fees. We don't. We pick the right platforms, run honest campaigns, and report in plain English.

Our story

Built around results,
not retainers.

We started NXT Media because we kept seeing small businesses get burned by agencies that charged thousands for "campaign management" and delivered vanity metrics — impressions, reach, "engagement" — none of which paid the rent.

So we built the agency we'd want to hire: month-to-month, transparent reporting, and pricing that scales with results. Lean enough to actually care about every campaign. Skilled enough to deliver.

What we believe

Three principles that decide every campaign we run:

01Cost per result is the only metric that matters. Impressions don't pay bills.
02You should own everything. Your ad accounts, your pixel, your domain — always in your name.
03If we're not delivering, you should be free to leave. No long contracts, ever.

How we work

The way an agency
should run.

Direct, not layered

You talk to the person running your campaigns. Not an account manager who relays your message to a junior buyer. Less telephone game, faster decisions.

Honest scope

We don't sell services you don't need. If TikTok isn't right for your business, we'll tell you on the strategy call — even if it loses us the deal.

Transparent fees

Flat monthly retainer. No commission on ad spend. No surprise charges. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Results documented

Monthly written report with the three numbers that matter: cost per result, click-through rate, and return on ad spend. Plus 24/7 access to your live dashboards.
