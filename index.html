FAQ

Common questions.

How fast will I see results?

Most clients see their first leads inside 7–14 days. Real optimization happens over the first 30–60 days as we kill underperforming ads and double down on the winners. Anyone promising overnight results is selling you something.

Do I need a big ad budget to start?

No. We recommend $300–500/month minimum on ad spend so the algorithms have enough data to optimize, but we've launched campaigns starting at $10/day. Ad spend is always your budget, paid directly to Meta/TikTok/Google — never to us.

What if I'm new to advertising?

Most of our clients are. We handle the full setup — ad accounts, pixel, tracking, creative — and walk you through what's happening in plain English. You don't need to learn ad jargon to get results.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Month-to-month, no minimum term. We give 30 days notice if either side wants to part ways — that's it. Long contracts protect agencies, not clients.

Who owns my ad account and website?

You do. Always. Your ad accounts, pixel, domain, and website stay in your name. We work inside your assets so if we ever part ways, your data and history stay with you.

What industries do you work with?

Local service businesses (restaurants, salons, gyms, fitness, home services) and e-commerce brands selling physical products. We don't take on regulated verticals like crypto, gambling, or supplements.

How is reporting handled?

Monthly written report with three numbers that matter: cost per result (CPR), click-through rate (CTR), and return on ad spend (ROAS). Plus access to your live ad account anytime — full transparency, always.
